ABOUT SKILLBRIDGE

How it works

A simple, student-powered way to exchange knowledge.

01
Tell us what you knowAdd the skills you feel comfortable teaching to another student.
02
Tell us what you want to learnChoose skills that match your interests, studies, or future goals.
03
Find a complementary partnerThe matching score rewards shared teach/learn interests in either direction.
04
Agree on a learning planSet a clear goal, choose a time, and keep each other accountable.

How matching works

This first version uses a transparent rule-based algorithm.

They can teach a skill I wantUp to 50 pts
I can teach a skill they wantUp to 35 pts
Shared interestsUp to 15 pts
The score is a compatibility estimate, not a judgement of a student's ability. The demo does not contact anyone or send messages.
